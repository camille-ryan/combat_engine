# Encounter Design Notes
This should potentially be moved to the eventual story engine module, but is useful for testing the combat engine. 

## Archetypal templates:

1. The Battlefield Control (The Trap)
- Composition: 1 Controller, 1 Soldier, 2 Skirmishers, 1 Lurker
- The Tactic: This was the baseline "smart" encounter. The Soldier holds the frontline, the Skirmishers flank, the Lurker strikes from the shadows, and the single Controller dictates the positioning of the PCs.
2. The Commander and Troops
- Composition: 1 Leader, 4-8 Minions, 2 Soldiers or Brutes
- The Tactic: Built to show enemy synergy. The Leader hangs back, using auras or active powers to buff the defenses of the Brutes or grant extra basic attacks to a massive swarm of Minions.
3. The Dragon's Den (The Solo)
- Composition: 1 Solo Monster (or 1 Solo + severe terrain traps)
- The Tactic: A true boss fight. The Solo monster possesses the raw XP weight and action economy of 5 standard monsters, forcing the party to coordinate entirely on a single massive threat.
4. The Wolf Pack
- Composition: 4-5 Skirmishers
- The Tactic: A high-octane, highly mobile fight. These monsters have abilities that trigger when they are adjacent to each other or when they shift, forcing the party's Defenders to constantly guess where the line of battle is.
5. The Double Line
- Composition: 2-3 Brutes or Soldiers, 2-3 Artillery
- The Tactic: A classic meat-shield dynamic. High-HP Brutes block the narrow hallway or choke point, while fragile but deadly Artillery units rain fire from absolute safety in the back.

## General notes:
Too many controllers can lock down the party and is anti fun.
Too many soldiers can be difficult to hit, and turn it into a miss fest.
Too many brutes can be just big hp sacks and turns into a slog. 
In general, lean on asymmetry. Use soldiers to protect artillery. Use controllers to enable the damage bonuses of lurkers. 

## Theming
Encounters read better if there are themes. All or mostly goblins, with a beast or two to support. Or a bugbear bruiser. A kobold or orcish warband. A pack of wolves. A dragon supported by its kobold minions.
