## ending, suspending, watching

- `c.arm_trigger(self, event: 'type[Event]', fn: 'Callable[[Any], None]', *, when: 'Callable[[Any], bool] | None' = None, cost: 'ActionType' = <ActionType.IMMEDIATE_REACTION: 'immediate_reaction'>, until: 'When' = <When.EONT: "end of source's next turn">, on: 'int | None' = None, window: 'Window | None' = None, label: 'str' = '') -> 'Effect'`

- `c.cancel(self) -> 'bool'`
